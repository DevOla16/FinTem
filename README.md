# FinTem Finance Tracker
### FinTem is an application I am building to track my income, expenses, budgets, financial reports and business spending.
___
# Description:
___
## What does FinTem do? 
### FinTem helps you track your transactions, expenses, budgets and generates proper financial documentation and summaries.
## Who is this for?
### FinTem is for individuals, businesses and business owners who want to manage, track, understand their spending better. It is also for individuals who want to generate GAAP compliant reports for their businesses, need secure and confidential AI tools to report errors in generated reports.
